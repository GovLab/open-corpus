## AI and democracy

- [Reboot: AI and the Race to Save Democracy](https://rebootdemocracy.ai/book) · book · Beth Simone Noveck's book on how AI can strengthen democratic institutions; the frame for much of Reboot Democracy's writing.
- [How A.I. Can Be Good for Democracy](https://rebootdemocracy.ai/blog/nyt-opinion-ai-democracy) · commentary, 2026 · Noveck's New York Times guest essay making the case for putting A.I. behind every public servant.
- [Doing Democracy with AI: Designing Public Engagement for the AI Era](https://rebootdemocracy.ai/blog/doing-democracy-ai) · commentary, 2026 · How institutions use AI and collective intelligence to engage the public at scale.
- [Learning to Listen: Why AI-Assisted Public Engagement Is a Skill We Need](https://rebootdemocracy.ai/blog/learning-to-listen-ai-public-engagement) · commentary, 2026 · Why listening to the public with AI is a skill governments now need.
- [Research Radar: The Emperor's New Agents - Why AI Won't Fix Broken Government](https://rebootdemocracy.ai/blog/research-radar-the-emperors-new-agents-why-ai-wont-fix-broken-government) · research review, 2025 · A critical reading of the "Agentic State" blueprint for rebuilding government around AI agents.

## Glossary

- **AI Localism**: how cities and local governments govern the use of AI, through their own policies, procurement and oversight. [AI Localism](https://ailocalism.org/), [AI Localism in Practice](https://files.thegovlab.org/ailocalism-in-practice.pdf)
- **Agentic State**: a blueprint, by others, for rebuilding government around AI agents that act and decide autonomously; Reboot Democracy reviews it critically. [The Emperor's New Agents](https://rebootdemocracy.ai/blog/research-radar-the-emperors-new-agents-why-ai-wont-fix-broken-government)
- **Collective intelligence**: drawing on the knowledge and skills of many people, inside and outside government, to solve problems. [Collective Intelligence case studies](https://collective-intelligence.thegovlab.org/), [Collective Crisis Intelligence](https://covidcourse.thegovlab.org/)
- **CrowdLaw**: using technology to involve the public in lawmaking, at any stage from defining problems to evaluating laws. [CrowdLaw Manifesto](https://manifesto.crowd.law/), [CrowdLaw Catalog](https://catalog.crowd.law/), [CrowdLaw for Congress](https://congress.crowd.law/)
- **Data collaborative**: a partnership in which private-sector data is shared, responsibly, for a public-interest purpose. [Data Collaboratives](https://datacollaboratives.org/), [Contracts for Data Collaboration](https://contractsfordatacollaboration.org/)
- **Data steward**: a person or team in a data-holding organisation whose role is to enable the responsible re-use of its data for the public good. [Wanted Data Stewards](https://www.thegovlab.org/static/files/publications/wanted-data-stewards.pdf)
- **Data responsibility**: handling data with care for rights and risks at every stage, from planning and collecting to analysing and using it. [Data Responsibility Journey](https://dataresponsibilityjourney.org/)
- **Digital self-determination**: the idea that people and communities should be able to determine how data about them is used; studied by an international network the GovLab is part of. [International Network on Digital Self-Determination](https://www.idsd.network/)
- **Third wave of open data**: the GovLab's term for a new phase of open data that focuses on re-using data for public-interest purposes while ensuring data rights and community flourishing. [The Third Wave of Open Data](https://blog.thegovlab.org/the-third-wave-of-open-data), [The Emergence of a Third Wave of Open Data](https://opendatapolicylab.org/images/odpl/third-wave-of-opendata.pdf), [Toolkit](https://files.thegovlab.org/The-Third-Wave-of-Open-Data-Toolkit.pdf)
- **The 100 Questions**: a method for agreeing, with experts, the questions in a field that data could and should answer. [The 100 Questions Initiative](https://the100questions.org/)
- **Data Assembly**: a public deliberation that gathers people's views on when and how data may be re-used, first run in New York City for crisis response. [The Data Assembly](https://thedataassembly.org/)
- **Smarter Crowdsourcing**: convening experts from around the world online to produce recommendations on one defined public problem. [Smarter Crowdsourcing](https://smartercrowdsourcing.org/)
- **Smarter State**: public institutions making better decisions by tapping the expertise of citizens and civil servants. [The Smarter State Case Studies](https://www.thegovlab.org/smarterstate)
- **Solving Public Problems**: the GovLab and Burnes Center approach to public problem-solving, from defining the problem to implementing a solution, taught in courses and worksheets. [Solving Public Problems](https://solvingpublicproblems.org/), [worksheets](https://worksheets.solvingpublicproblems.org/)
- **Selected Readings**: a GovLab blog series of curated readings on one topic; search the govlab-blog files for "Selected Readings". Example: [Selected Readings: The Potential of PeaceTech](https://blog.thegovlab.org/selected-readings-the-potential-of-peacetech)
- **News That Caught Our Eye / Reboot Weekly**: Reboot Democracy's weekly digest of news on AI, democracy and governance; every edition is in news-digests.md.
- **Research Radar**: a Reboot Democracy series reviewing new research on AI and government. Example: [The Emperor's New Agents](https://rebootdemocracy.ai/blog/research-radar-the-emperors-new-agents-why-ai-wont-fix-broken-government)
- **Global AI Watch**: a Reboot Democracy series on how governments around the world use AI. Example: [Listening to Public Servants: What Dubai and New Jersey Teach Us About AI Readiness](https://rebootdemocracy.ai/blog/global-ai-watch-listening-to-public-servants-what-dubai-and-new-jersey-teach-us-about-ai-readiness)
- **Pi-Squared**: Private Innovation in the Public Interest, Anita McGahan's video interviews with scholars on how private innovation can serve the public interest. [Pi-Squared](https://pisquared.theburnescenter.org/)

## Who's who

- **The GovLab** (The Governance Lab): founded by Beth Simone Noveck; research on governance, data and collective intelligence. Its case studies, publications, projects, courses and blog are in the govlab-*.md files.
- **Burnes Center for Social Change**: at Northeastern University, directed by Beth Simone Noveck; home of AI for Impact and Pi-Squared.
- **Reboot Democracy** (rebootdemocracy.ai): explores how AI, if designed and governed wisely, can strengthen democratic institutions; edited by Beth Simone Noveck with colleagues at The GovLab and the Burnes Center, and a partner project of InnovateUS. In blog-posts.md and news-digests.md.
- **InnovateUS** (innovate-us.org): free at-your-own-pace and live learning on data, digital, innovation and AI skills for public service professionals; a partner project of the Burnes Center, The GovLab, Reboot Democracy and AI for Impact. In workshops.md, courses.md and innovateus-articles.md.
- **AI for Impact**: Northeastern co-op programme where students build AI tools with government partners. [AI for Impact](https://ai4impact.ai/)
