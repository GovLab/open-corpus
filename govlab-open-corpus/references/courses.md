- [Designing Democratic Engagement with AI](https://innovate-us.org/course/democratic-engagement-for-ai-era) · This free, self-paced course equips you with the skills to design, run and learn from meaningful engagement with communities.

- [Responsible AI for Public Organizations](https://innovate-us.org/course/responsible-ai-for-public-organizations) · This two-part course guides public sector professionals through building impactful, responsible AI initiatives. - [The Innovation Skills Accelerator.](https://innovate-us.org/course/innovation-skills-accelerator) · Based on research with thousands of public servants across three continents, our problem-solving curriculum gives you the digital, data and innovation skills to deliver more effective, equitable and engaged policies and services.
- [What Works: Fast Field Scanning with AI](https://innovate-us.org/course/what-works-fast-field-scanning-with-ai) · This course is designed to help public professionals accelerate the process of finding and implementing urgently-needed evidence-based solutions to public problems.
